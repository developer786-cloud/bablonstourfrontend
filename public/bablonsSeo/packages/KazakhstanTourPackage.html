Package Overview

Almaty & Kolsai Lakes, 6 Days 5 Nights from India

The Almaty & Kolsai Lakes Kazakhstan Tour Package is a 6-day, 5-night group holiday designed for Indian travellers exploring Central Asia for the first time. It covers 4 nights in Almaty plus 1 night at a resort in the Kolsai Lake region, daily breakfast and dinner, airport meet & greet with return transfers, and transportation for the full tour. Sightseeing includes the Shymbulak Mountain and Ski Resort gondola ride, Panfilov Park, Zenkov Cathedral and Arbat Street in Almaty; Black Canyon on the way to Kolsai Lake; Kaindy Lake's famous sunken forest and Charyn Canyon's Valley of Castles; and a day of local flavours, shopping and skyline views from Kok Tobe Hill back in Almaty. The tour runs on a group basis of 18–25 travellers with an English-speaking guide, and is available in a Classic (3★ hotel) or Premium (4★ hotel) category. International airfare from India is not included and can be arranged separately on request.

Destination Guide

Getting to Know Almaty & the Kolsai Lake Region

About Almaty

Almaty is Kazakhstan's cultural and economic capital, set against the Tien Shan mountains, blending Soviet-era architecture with a modern, green city.

Kolsai, Kaindy & Charyn

A cluster of natural wonders 3–4 hours from Almaty: alpine Kolsai Lake, the sunken forest of Kaindy Lake, and the red-rock Charyn Canyon.

Best Time to Visit

March–June and September–November generally bring the most pleasant weather for sightseeing and mountain excursions; December–February suits winter sports at Shymbulak.

Currency & Language

The local currency is the Kazakhstani tenge. Kazakh and Russian are widely spoken; English is used in tourism-facing services.

Travel Tip

Carry your original passport while visiting the Kolsai and Kaindy Lakes region, as it lies close to the Kyrgyzstan border and may involve checks.

Packing Tip

Pack layered clothing and comfortable trekking shoes — temperatures can drop quickly in the mountains even when Almaty city is warm.
