Entry Requirements

Kazakhstan Visa for Indians — What You Actually Need

Indian passport holders get 14 days visa-free entry

Since 2022, Indian citizens can enter Kazakhstan without a visa for tourism, private visits, and short-term business, for up to 14 consecutive days per visit. Within any rolling 180-day period, your total visa-free stay cannot exceed 42 days.

You do not need to apply for anything in advance for a stay of 14 days or less — simply arrive with a valid passport (recommended 6 months’ validity remaining), your return/onward flight ticket, and proof of hotel booking.

Need longer than 14 days? Kazakhstan offers an e-Visa for Indian citizens allowing stays of up to 30 days (tourist) or 60 days (business), applied for online through the official visa portal before travel. Our 6 Days / 5 Nights Almaty & Kolsai Lakes package comfortably fits within the 14-day visa-free window, so most travellers on this itinerary won’t need to apply for anything extra.

Visa policies can change without much notice. Please reconfirm the latest Kazakhstan entry requirements with the Kazakhstan Embassy in India or your Bablons travel consultant before booking flights, especially if you are combining this trip with other Central Asian countries.
When to Go

Best Time to Visit Kazakhstan & Almaty

Kazakhstan has a continental climate with sharply defined seasons, so the “best” time really depends on what you want to do — mountain sightseeing and lake visits, or snow and skiing.

Mar – May

Spring. Snow starts melting, mild days, great for city sightseeing and early mountain visits.

Jun – Aug

Summer. Warm 20–35°C, ideal for Kolsai Lake, Kaindy Lake and Charyn Canyon excursions.

Sep – Nov

Autumn. Cool, clear skies and golden landscapes — many travellers rate this as the most scenic season.

Dec – Feb

Winter. Cold with heavy snow; ideal for Shymbulak’s ski season, less ideal for the Kolsai/Charyn excursion.

For a first Kazakhstan trip that covers both Almaty city and the Kolsai Lake/Charyn Canyon region, March–June and September–November are generally the best windows — mild enough for the mountain excursion, with the sunken forest and canyon fully accessible.

Where to Go

Top Places to Visit in Kazakhstan (Almaty Region)

City

Almaty

Kazakhstan’s cultural capital, set against the Tien Shan mountains, with Panfilov Park, Zenkov Cathedral and lively Arbat Street.

Mountains

Shymbulak Ski Resort

A gondola ride up into the Tien Shan range for panoramic alpine views, popular in every season.

Lake

Kolsai Lake

Crystal-clear alpine water ringed by pine forest — often called the “Pearl of the Tien Shan.”

Natural Wonder

Kaindy Lake

A surreal sunken forest with dead spruce trunks rising straight out of turquoise water.

Canyon

Charyn Canyon

The dramatic red-rock Valley of Castles, frequently compared to a mini Grand Canyon.

Viewpoint

Kok Tobe Hill

A gondola ride above Almaty for sweeping city and mountain skyline views.

Market

Green Bazaar

A colourful local market and the famous Rakhat Chocolate Shop for Kazakhstan’s signature sweets.

Scenic Route

Black Canyon

A panoramic viewpoint stop on the drive between Almaty and the Kolsai Lake region.

Trip Length

How Many Days Do You Need for a Kazakhstan Trip?

Most travellers find 5 to 6 days enough to comfortably cover Almaty city, the Shymbulak gondola, and a full excursion to the Kolsai Lake, Kaindy Lake and Charyn Canyon region without feeling rushed. Shorter 3–4 day trips usually have to drop either the Kolsai Lake overnight or the Kaindy/Charyn day, so if seeing all four natural landmarks matters to you, plan for at least 6 days.

Before You Go

Practical Travel Tips for Indians Visiting Kazakhstan

Currency

Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT). ATMs are widely available in Almaty. Carry some cash for mountain areas and local markets. Major international cards work in cities.

Language

Kazakh and Russian are the main languages. English is spoken in hotels, major tourist spots and by many younger people in Almaty. A translation app is useful.

Local SIM / Internet

Local SIM cards (Beeline, Tele2, Kcell) are easy to buy at the airport or in the city. Many hotels and cafes offer free Wi-Fi.

Packing

Layered clothing is essential. Comfortable walking shoes for city and mountain trails. A light jacket even in summer evenings near the lakes.

Documents

Original passport with recommended 6 months validity, return flight ticket, and hotel confirmation. Keep digital and physical copies.

Safety & Health

Almaty and the popular tourist areas are generally safe. Travel insurance is strongly recommended. Check any current health advisories before departure.
