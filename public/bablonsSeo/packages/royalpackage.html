Package Overview

Tashkent Royal, 5 Days 4 Nights

The Tashkent Royal Package is a 5-day, 4-night luxury group tour built for travellers who want more comfort and more inclusions than a standard Tashkent holiday. It includes a 5-star hotel stay, daily breakfast, lunch and a lavish dinner with complimentary spirits and beverages, Uzbekistan visa fees and assistance, a complimentary local SIM card, Delhi pickup and drop, airport meet & greet and private transfers, and transportation throughout the tour. The sightseeing programme is extensive: Tashkent city landmarks including the Khast Imam Complex, Kukeldash Madrasah, Tashkent Metro, Amir Temur Museum, Independence Square, Tashkent City Park, Magic City, Magic Aquarium, Tashkent TV Tower, Alisher Navoi Opera & Ballet Theatre and the Lal Bahadur Shastri Statue, plus a Chimgan Mountains and Charvak Lake excursion with the Chimgan/Amirsoy cable car, and a shopping tour covering Tashkent City Mall, Mega City Mall, Sayilgoh Street, Chorsu Bazaar, Samarkand Darvoza Mall and Atlas Mall. A tour manager accompanies the group of 20–25 travellers, with 2 departures scheduled per month according to current package data. International airfare is not included.

Destination Guide

Getting to Know Tashkent

About Tashkent

Tashkent is the capital of Uzbekistan, mixing Soviet-era architecture, an old city of madrasahs and mosques, and a growing modern skyline.

The Tashkent Metro

Several stations are known for elaborate, palace-like decoration and are a sightseeing attraction in their own right.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) generally bring milder weather for sightseeing; summers can be hot and winters cold.

Shopping in Tashkent

From the historic Chorsu Bazaar to modern malls like Tashkent City Mall and Atlas Mall, the city offers a wide shopping range.

Currency & Language

The local currency is the Uzbekistani som. Uzbek and Russian are widely spoken; English is used in tourism-facing services.

Evening Etiquette

Complimentary spirits are served with dinner as part of this package; let your tour manager know of any dietary or beverage preferences in advance.
