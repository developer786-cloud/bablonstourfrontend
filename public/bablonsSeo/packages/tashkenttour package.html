Destination Guide

Getting to Know Tashkent

About Tashkent

Tashkent is the capital of Uzbekistan and the largest city in Central Asia, blending Soviet-era architecture, a historic old city and modern developments.

Chimgan & Charvak

Roughly a day trip from Tashkent, this mountain and lake region is a popular escape from the city for scenery and cooler air.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) generally bring milder weather for sightseeing; summers can be hot and winters cold, so pack accordingly.

Currency & Language

The local currency is the Uzbekistani som. Uzbek and Russian are widely spoken; English is used in tourism-facing services.

Travel Tip

Carry a copy of your passport and visa documents while sightseeing, and keep some local currency handy for entrance tickets that aren't included in the package.

Packing Tip

Comfortable walking shoes and layered clothing help for both city sightseeing and the Chimgan mountain excursion.
