Destination Guide

Getting to Know Tashkent

About Tashkent

Tashkent is the capital of Uzbekistan and the largest city in Central Asia, blending Soviet-era architecture, a historic old city and modern developments.

Chimgan & Charvak

A popular day-trip region of mountains and a reservoir outside Tashkent, often included in Uzbekistan itineraries.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) generally bring milder weather for sightseeing; summers can be hot and winters cold.

Currency & Language

The local currency is the Uzbekistani som. Uzbek and Russian are widely spoken; English is used in tourism-facing services.

Getting Around

Guided group transport is typical for organised tours; the ornately decorated Tashkent Metro is also worth experiencing.

Travel Tip

Since your itinerary is customised, share your must-see list early so the plan can be built around it rather than adjusted later.
